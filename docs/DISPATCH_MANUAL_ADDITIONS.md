# Delivery and quality-case manual additions

These workflows store operational evidence through `core.execute`. They never create stock movements, revenue, customer credits, refunds, payroll, or expenses. The original sale/transfer remains the authoritative accounting and inventory event. Screens are available in the transport and customer quality workspace. Vehicle, driver, trip, load, acknowledgement, claim, decision, and recall records are business scoped.

## Important operating limits

- Transport acknowledgement is evidence about already posted sale/dispatch quantities. Current sales recognize delivery using the existing counter-sale policy. This module does not introduce deferred-revenue or unbilled-delivery accounting.
- Same-company stock physically dispatched to Kabul still requires the existing `transfer_receive` workflow. A delivery acknowledgement is not warehouse receipt.
- A driver's or dispatcher’s location membership governs transport access. A trip can contain several source documents; access to the whole trip requires access to every loaded document and product line. No partial financial-document disclosure is used.
- Claims do not make warranty or legal-entitlement decisions automatically. Notes, private photographs, inspection records, and independently authorized returns/replacements preserve the review trail.
- A recalled pooled adhesive/count product is conservatively held because the existing moving-average sale pool does not prove exact finished-batch allocation. This is deliberately broader than an exact block recall.
- Recall coverage includes only recorded material-lot usage, production completions, and block descendants. Legacy untracked raw materials are not represented as fully traceable.
- Recall closure leaves inspection/curing/damage holds intact. Even a block that was ready before the recall needs a separate quality release. A material lot needs a separate reviewed `lot_status` release.
- Ordinary sale, cutting, quality release, material issue/consumption, transfers, and adjustments cannot bypass active block/product recall guards. Unsupported movement of recalled stock fails explicitly; review and end the campaign before using those normal workflows. Nothing is silently written off.
- Uploaded evidence is private and remains subject to the source document’s business/location permissions. No SMS, email, customer notification, or external message is sent by a recall or claim.

## Permissions

Transport: `delivery.view`, `delivery.manage`, `delivery.post`, `delivery.confirm`, `delivery.approve`. Quality cases: `quality.view`, `quality.claim`, `quality.approve`. Business-wide campaigns: `quality.recall`, full location scope, and access to every affected product line. Customer follow-up contacts additionally require `sales.view` or `quality.recall_customers`. Photo/document upload additionally requires `attachments.create`.

Every posted operation retains its original immutable source document and actor. A retry with the same idempotency key returns that original event. A conflicting payload fails. Database tenant guards protect referenced records, and delivery acknowledgements/load/case-event source records become immutable after posting.
