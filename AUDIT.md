## Reading the tables

**✅ EXISTS** means the stated behavior is already supported and evidenced; preserve it. **🟡 PARTIAL** means the overall acceptance criteria are not met, even when the underlying accounting behavior is correct. **❌ MISSING** means the requested capability was not found after searching the relevant models, registry, services, routes, UI and tests. **❓ UNVERIFIABLE** means a specific external fact or physical observation remains unproven, not that it should be rewritten.

Evidence references are repository-relative `file:line` at this snapshot; referenced blocks often continue beyond that line. For absent features the evidence identifies the actual neighboring implementation and what was searched. Effort is a preliminary **engineering-days range per row**, including focused tests/localization/docs; it excludes owner decisions, accountant approval, devices and third-party delivery. Rows overlap: P0-2/F, P1-4/F, P0-4/P2-5 and G/P2-10 must share work rather than be counted twice. High risk means accounting/security/history impact, not proof of an existing financial error.
